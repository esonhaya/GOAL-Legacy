# GOAL: Legacy
## Project Manifesto

> Every football career should become a story worth remembering.

---

## Why GOAL: Legacy Exists

GOAL: Legacy exists to create a football career simulation where every decision matters, every save creates its own history, and every player leaves a legacy.

Rather than recreating football as it happened, GOAL: Legacy creates believable alternate football universes that evolve independently from the player.

The objective is not perfect realism.

The objective is believable football lives.

---

## Our Principles

### Every Save Is Unique

No two careers should unfold in exactly the same way.

---

### The World Lives Without You

Clubs, players, managers, media, and supporters continue making decisions whether the player succeeds or fails.

---

### Failure Creates Stories

Losing a final, suffering an injury, or becoming a forgotten talent should create memorable careers—not broken saves.

---

### Money Always Has Purpose

Wealth should unlock meaningful opportunities, responsibilities, and choices throughout an entire career.

---

### Football Comes First

Every feature must strengthen the football simulation before adding complexity.

---

### Small Beginnings, Endless Possibilities

Every career begins with an unknown youngster.

Greatness is earned through decisions, performances, relationships, and perseverance.

---

### Build in Phases

GOAL: Legacy grows through carefully planned phases.

Every phase must be stable, playable, and enjoyable before the next begins.

---

## Final Goal

Create a football universe that players remember long after they stop playing.

A game where people don't just remember the trophies they won...

They remember the life they lived.

---

*"Every legend begins as someone nobody knows."*
