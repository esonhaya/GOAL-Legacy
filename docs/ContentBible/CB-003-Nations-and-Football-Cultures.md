# Purpose

This document defines how nations develop unique football identities within GOAL: Legacy.

Rather than making every country feel identical, each nation should gradually develop its own football culture, strengths, traditions, and reputation through long-term simulation.

---

# Philosophy

Football culture is not static.

It evolves through:

- Player development
- Coaching philosophies
- League quality
- International success
- Historical achievements

The simulation should allow nations to grow, decline, or reinvent themselves over time.

---

# National Identity

Each nation possesses characteristics such as:

- Football reputation
- Youth development quality
- Tactical preferences
- League prestige
- International competitiveness
- Export strength
- Domestic investment

These values influence, but never completely determine, future outcomes.

---

# Football Culture

Football culture reflects how the game is commonly played within a nation.

Examples include:

- Attacking emphasis
- Defensive organization
- Technical development
- Physical intensity
- Tactical discipline
- Creative freedom

These tendencies emerge over time rather than acting as rigid rules.

---

# Youth Development

Each nation develops players according to its football environment.

Factors may include:

- Coaching quality
- Investment
- Facilities
- Competition level
- Historical success

Exceptional individuals may emerge regardless of national averages.

---

# League Reputation

League reputation influences:

- International attractiveness
- Player transfers
- Financial opportunities
- Global visibility

League prestige should change naturally as clubs succeed or decline.

---

# International Football

National teams represent the highest level of football achievement.

International success contributes to:

- National prestige
- Player reputation
- Youth participation
- Long-term football culture

Strong generations may permanently influence future development.

---

# Dynamic Evolution

National football should never remain fixed.

Examples:

- Emerging football nations
- Declining historic powers
- Tactical revolutions
- Investment-driven growth
- Golden generations

Every save should produce a unique football history.

---

# Diversity

GOAL: Legacy celebrates diversity in football.

Different nations should feel unique without relying on simplistic stereotypes.

Identity should emerge from the simulation rather than predetermined narratives.

---

# Future Expansion

Future versions may include:

- Regional playing styles
- Football academies
- Weather influence
- Fan traditions
- Language support
- National coaching schools
- Domestic football policies

These additions should deepen national identity without changing the core philosophy.

---

# Locked Decisions

✓ Nations evolve over time.

✓ Football cultures are dynamic.

✓ Identity influences development.

✓ Exceptional players can emerge anywhere.

✓ International success shapes the future.

✓ Diversity strengthens the simulation.
