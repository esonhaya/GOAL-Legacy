# Purpose

This document defines the long-term identity of football clubs within GOAL: Legacy.

A club is more than its current squad or league position. It possesses history, culture, ambitions, traditions, and expectations that persist across generations.

---

# Philosophy

Clubs should feel alive.

Even after decades of simulation, supporters should still recognize what makes their club unique.

Identity should outlive individual players, managers, and owners.

---

# Club Identity

Every club possesses a unique identity made up of multiple characteristics.

Examples include:

- History
- Reputation
- Tradition
- Supporter culture
- Long-term ambitions
- Football philosophy
- Financial profile
- Youth philosophy

Identity evolves gradually over time.

---

# Club DNA

Club DNA represents the football principles traditionally associated with the club.

Examples include:

- Attacking football
- Defensive discipline
- Technical possession
- High pressing
- Counter-attacking
- Youth development
- Buying established stars
- Financial sustainability

Club DNA influences long-term decision making but never completely restricts it.

---

# Club Culture

Every club develops its own internal culture.

Examples:

- Winning mentality
- Family atmosphere
- Professional discipline
- Player loyalty
- Manager stability
- Community involvement

Culture changes slowly and reflects decades of history.

---

# Supporters

Supporters are central to club identity.

Supporters develop expectations based on:

- Club history
- Rivals
- League position
- Recent performances
- Style of football
- Youth development

Supporter expectations influence the long-term perception of success.

---

# Rivalries

Rivalries emerge through:

- Geography
- Historic matches
- Competition battles
- Political or cultural history
- Repeated encounters

Some rivalries are historic.

Others may emerge naturally during simulation.

---

# Ambition

Club ambition changes over time.

Examples include:

- Avoid relegation
- Qualify for continental football
- Win domestic titles
- Develop academy players
- Become a global powerhouse

Ambition should respond to long-term success and failure.

---

# Reputation

Club reputation reflects worldwide recognition.

It is influenced by:

- Trophies
- International success
- Financial stability
- Legendary players
- Historic achievements

Reputation should grow or decline naturally.

---

# Legacy

Every club leaves a legacy.

Examples include:

- Legendary managers
- Iconic players
- Historic seasons
- Famous victories
- Record-breaking achievements

Legacy becomes part of football history.

---

# Dynamic Evolution

Club identity is never completely fixed.

Examples:

- New ownership
- Golden generations
- Financial collapse
- Tactical revolutions
- Stadium expansion

Major events may reshape a club while preserving its history.

---

# Future Expansion

Future versions may include:

- Club museums
- Club philosophies by era
- Board personalities
- Ultra groups
- Fan organizations
- Stadium traditions
- Community projects

These additions should deepen club identity without replacing its core principles.

---

✓ Culture evolves slowly.

✓ Legacy outlives generations.
