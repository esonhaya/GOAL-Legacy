# Purpose

This document defines how people are represented within GOAL: Legacy beyond their technical abilities.

Every individual possesses a unique identity shaped by personality, experiences, ambitions, relationships, and personal history.

The objective is to ensure that football careers feel human rather than purely statistical.

---

# Philosophy

No two people should feel identical.

Two players with identical technical abilities may make completely different career decisions because they value different things.

People are remembered for who they are, not only for what they can do.

---

# Who Are "People"

The term "people" includes:

- Players
- Managers
- Coaches
- Scouts
- Medical staff
- Executives
- Referees (future)
- Agents (future)
- Journalists (future)

All intelligent individuals share the same behavioural foundation.

---

# Personality

Personality influences long-term behaviour.

Examples include:

- Ambition
- Loyalty
- Professionalism
- Leadership
- Confidence
- Patience
- Risk tolerance
- Discipline
- Adaptability

Personality changes slowly throughout a career.

---

# Ambition

Ambition shapes career goals.

Examples:

- Become a club legend
- Win international trophies
- Earn a high salary
- Play in stronger leagues
- Develop young players
- Build a dynasty

Ambition evolves through experience.

---

# Reputation

Reputation reflects how the football world views an individual.

It may be influenced by:

- Performance
- Consistency
- Awards
- Leadership
- Conduct
- Historic achievements

Reputation changes over time.

---

# Relationships

People develop meaningful relationships.

Examples:

- Friendships
- Mentorships
- Rivalries
- Professional respect
- Club loyalty

Relationships influence future decisions but never completely determine them.

---

# Personal History

Every career builds a unique history.

Examples:

- Academy graduate
- Journeyman
- One-club legend
- Late bloomer
- Wonderkid
- Veteran leader

History should shape future opportunities.

---

# Growth

People continue evolving.

Examples:

- Increased leadership
- Improved professionalism
- Greater confidence
- Changing ambitions

Development extends beyond football ability.

---

# Identity

Every person should be identifiable through:

- Career path
- Personality
- Achievements
- Relationships
- Legacy

Identity becomes stronger as careers progress.

---

# Future Expansion

Future versions may include:

- Family backgrounds
- Languages
- Cultural influences
- Public image
- Lifestyle
- Personal interests
- Post-retirement careers

These additions should deepen immersion without changing the core philosophy.

---

# Locked Decisions

✓ Every person is unique.

✓ Personality influences behaviour.

✓ Relationships matter.

✓ Careers create identity.

✓ Growth extends beyond attributes.

✓ People leave lasting legacies.
