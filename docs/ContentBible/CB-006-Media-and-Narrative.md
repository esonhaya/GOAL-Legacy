Title: Media and Narrative

Version: 1.0
Status: Approved Blueprint

---

# Purpose

This document defines how football stories are communicated within GOAL: Legacy.

Media exists to inform the player, celebrate achievements, record history, and strengthen immersion without becoming the driving force behind the simulation.

Football creates stories.

Media tells them.

---

# Philosophy

Media should reflect the football world rather than control it.

Every article, headline, interview, or future social interaction should originate from genuine events created by the simulation.

Authenticity takes priority over sensationalism.

---

# Storytelling

GOAL: Legacy favors emergent storytelling.

Examples include:

- A club's unexpected rise
- A legendary retirement
- A record-breaking transfer
- A youth academy success story
- A manager rebuilding a fallen giant
- A fierce rivalry spanning decades

These stories are discovered rather than scripted.

---

# News

News serves as the primary source of football information.

Coverage includes:

- Match reports
- Transfers
- Injuries
- Awards
- Competition updates
- Financial news
- Club announcements
- Historic milestones

News should vary in importance according to the significance of the event.

---

# Narrative

Narratives emerge over time.

Examples include:

- Golden generations
- Club dynasties
- Underdog stories
- Relegation battles
- Record chases
- Historic rivalries

Narratives connect many individual events into memorable football stories.

---

# Historical Memory

Important stories should remain accessible.

Examples:

- Season reviews
- Historic transfers
- Legendary matches
- Hall of Fame inductions
- Club anniversaries

The world should remember meaningful moments.

---

# Tone

Media should remain:

- Professional
- Believable
- Informative
- Football-focused

Writing style may differ between publications in future versions.

---

# Player Experience

Media exists to help the player understand the evolving football world.

It should:

- Highlight important events
- Explain context
- Encourage exploration
- Celebrate history
- Create emotional attachment

Media should never overwhelm the player with unnecessary information.

---

# Future Expansion

Future versions may introduce:

- Social platforms
- Journalists
- Interviews
- Press conferences
- Television broadcasts
- Podcasts
- Fan communities
- Regional media outlets

Each addition should strengthen immersion without changing the philosophy established in this document.

---

# Locked Decisions

✓ Football creates stories.

✓ Media reports stories.

✓ Narratives emerge naturally.

✓ History remains accessible.

✓ Authenticity comes before spectacle.

✓ Media supports the simulation rather than replacing it.
