# Purpose

This document defines how GOAL: Legacy preserves football history across generations.

The objective is to ensure that every meaningful achievement, rivalry, club, player, manager, and competition contributes to a living historical record that grows with every season.

History is not decoration.

History is gameplay.

---

# Philosophy

Every save creates a unique football universe.

The purpose of the Legacy system is not to recreate real-world football history forever, but to allow new history to emerge naturally through simulation.

The greatest legends of one save may never exist in another.

---

# Living History

The football world remembers.

Examples include:

- Historic title races
- Legendary finals
- Record-breaking transfers
- Club dynasties
- Great managers
- Golden generations
- Unexpected underdogs

History should influence how the world is perceived.

---

# Legacy

Legacy represents lasting significance.

It is earned over years rather than individual moments.

Legacy may belong to:

- Players
- Managers
- Clubs
- Competitions
- Nations

Legacy should outlive active careers.

---

# Hall of Fame

The Hall of Fame celebrates individuals whose achievements permanently shaped football history.

Selection should consider:

- Career achievements
- Longevity
- Influence
- Historic moments
- Reputation

Hall of Fame induction should feel prestigious rather than routine.

# Record Books

The football world maintains permanent records.

Examples:

- Most goals
- Most appearances
- Most assists
- Most trophies
- Biggest transfer fee
- Longest unbeaten run

Records provide historical context for future generations.

## Player Career Legacy presentation

The controlled Player's Legacy is a compact retrospective, not an achievement
grind. It may show the Career span, represented Clubs, Club and international
totals, competition honours, individual Season awards, personal best Seasons,
and durable numeric milestones. Copy should name the football achievement
itself — for example, “League Champion” or “Top Scorer” — rather than expose a
synthetic points balance.

Annual recognition is intentionally small: Player of the Season, Young Player
of the Season, Top Scorer, and Top Assist Provider are domestic-League facts
when the completed Season contains sufficient evidence. World-fidelity NPCs
can appear as winners from compact aggregates, but the game does not invent
their detailed Match histories. A trophy is described only when participation
evidence supports the Player's attribution.

# Competition History

Competitions preserve:

- Champions
- Finals
- Historic upsets
- Records

---

# Timelines

The simulation should maintain an accessible historical timeline.

Examples:

- Major transfers
- Record-breaking seasons
- Legendary retirements
- Hall of Fame inductions
- Club milestones

Players should be able to revisit the past.

---

# Player Experience

History should reward long-term saves.

The longer a save continues, the richer the football world becomes.

Players should eventually recognize names that never existed before starting their save.

---

# Future Expansion

Future versions may include:

- Club museums
- Interactive timelines
- Documentary mode
- Historical anniversaries
- "On This Day" memories
- Greatest XI selections
- Community-voted legends

These additions should deepen immersion while preserving the philosophy established here.
