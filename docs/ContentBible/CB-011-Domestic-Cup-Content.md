## Competition content

The initial content set contains five generic domestic Cups:

| Nation | Competition | Eligible Clubs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| England | English Domestic Cup | existing English first and second tiers |
| Spain | Spanish Domestic Cup | existing Spanish first and second tiers |
| Germany | German Domestic Cup | existing German first and second tiers |
| Italy | Italian Domestic Cup | existing Italian first and second tiers |
| France | French Domestic Cup | existing French first and second tiers |

These names are generic presentation names. No licensed logos, branding, or
real-world lower-division Clubs are required.

## Narrative content

Cup-facing career moments are uncommon and football-centered. Initial moments
cover a coming knockout tie, a first Cup appearance, advancement, a rotation
opportunity, later-round pressure, a Cup run, and elimination. Choices should
offer preparation, recovery, professional, and lifestyle tradeoffs without
inventing reputation meters or Cup-specific attributes.

News is reserved for controlled-Club results, notable advancement, finals,
winners, and meaningful Player moments. Routine NPC round results remain
available on the Cup page without flooding the News feed.

## Presentation language

Use “domestic cup”, “knockout round”, “advanced”, “eliminated”, “final”, and
“winner” in player-facing copy. Shootout scores are clearly labeled as
penalties and never described as Match goals. Avoid raw competition IDs,
internal state names, and promises of Europe or international qualification.
