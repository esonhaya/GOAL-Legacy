# European Club Content

Document ID: CB-012
Title: European Club Competition Content and Tone
Status: Phase 2 content contract

## Scope

The V1 world contains two generic, unlicensed Club competitions:

| ID | Player-facing identity | Field |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `europe-tier-1` | Continental Champions Competition | 16 existing Big-5 Clubs |
| `europe-tier-2` | Continental Challenge Competition | 16 different existing Big-5 Clubs |

The field is deliberately smaller than a real-world confederation database.
No Clubs from currently unsimulated countries are invented. The content uses a
schema-compatible nation anchor, while competition type and tier define the
continental identity. No real UEFA branding, logos, coefficients, or prize
money are implemented.

## Narrative

Selective News and Career Experience moments cover qualification, a European
debut, group pressure, a major knockout night, elimination, a first final, and
championship celebration. Routine NPC group scores remain on the competition
page and do not flood News. European final and penalty language is explicit;
shootout attempts are never described as normal Match goals.

## Future boundary

Domestic League/Cup success determines European opportunity from Season 2
onward. International football, national teams, European coefficient history,
and additional continental tiers remain future content and are not implied by
this document.
