Title: Generic National-Team and World Championship Content
Status: Phase 2 content contract

## Supported identity

The V1 world presents generic national teams for England, France, Germany,
Italy, Scotland, Spain, and Wales because these are the selected Nations with
real Player populations in the current generated world. Names are descriptive
(``England National Team`` and equivalent); no federation crests, licensed
kits, FIFA branding, or real competition marks are used. Player portraits and
Club identities continue to come from the existing content systems.

## Competition

`World Championship` is the generic world-level national-team competition.
It is a six-team, two-group, single-round-robin tournament in the current V1
world, followed by semi-finals and a final. The field is intentionally based
on available Players rather than fabricated global coverage. Initial entries
use deterministic national-team strength; later entries cite completed
domestic results with deterministic strength fallback. This is not a global
qualifying simulation.

## Narrative tone

International News and Career Events emphasize sporting opportunity: first
call-up, camp, debut, first goal, tournament pressure, advancement,
elimination, final, and championship. Avoid political, nationalistic, or
stereotyped writing. Routine NPC group results remain compact world facts.

Future continental national tournaments, national youth football, dual
nationality, and broader country coverage require a separate content decision.
